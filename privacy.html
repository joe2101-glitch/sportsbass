Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 23, 2026

SportsBass AI ("we", "our", "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

SportsBass AI is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

• No personal information: We do not require account creation, login, or any personal details to use the App.
• No tracking: We do not use analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking tools.
• Usage data: We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics through Apple's standard App Analytics (if you have opted into sharing data with developers via your iOS settings). This includes: device type, OS version, app launches, and crash reports. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

2. Data Storage

All predictions and matchup selections are processed locally on your device. No prediction data, team selections, or usage patterns are transmitted to our servers. Statistical model data (team stats, league data) is fetched from publicly hosted static files and cached locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The App fetches statistical data from GitHub-hosted JSON files. These requests are standard HTTPS GET requests and do not transmit any personal or identifying information. We do not use any third-party advertising, analytics, or social media SDKs.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 17+ due to its sports prediction and statistical odds content. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 17. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us and we will take steps to remove that information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties. Period.

6. Your Rights

Since we collect virtually no personal data, there is minimal data to manage. However, you have the right to: (a) opt out of Apple's App Analytics sharing in your iOS Settings; (b) delete the App at any time, which removes all locally cached data; (c) contact us with any privacy concerns.

7. Data Security

All data transfers use HTTPS encryption. Local data is stored within the App's sandboxed environment following Apple's security standards.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of any changes.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: sportsbassai@gmail.com
